## Criteria the agent decided

These were not in the issues, so the agent decided them, and they are written in chapters 4 to 7 of `docs/decisions/schedule-rules.md`. The user has not confirmed them yet.

- After creation, a schedule can only have its name edited. The start date is today or later, the period is at most 366 days, and the end date is at most the end of next year.
- If you un-complete an item with a past date, only that item moves to today.
- Progress is the target time of finished items ÷ the total target time.
- A completed chapter is one with a completed study record in the chapter or in a section under it.
- The achievement and adherence rates are counted by the originally planned dates. Even if you miss only the first day and then finish every day, the adherence rate can drop to 0%.
