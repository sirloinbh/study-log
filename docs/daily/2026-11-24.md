## Built

- **#366 Google Calendar sync (PR #398).** Schedule days and review due dates become all-day events in a separate "Study log" calendar and move or disappear when the app changes them. One-way: nothing is read back from Google. It is a reconciler, not hooks: every sync compares what the app holds with the events made before and fixes the difference, so a missed trigger is repaired by the next sync. Plain `fetch` to Google, no new package; the refresh token is encrypted with a key from the environment. Settings page card to connect, sync now and disconnect. Migration 033. Details: `docs/changes/366-google-calendar-sync.md`.
- **#403 Exercise reviews in the calendar sync (PR #407).** The 1, 3, 7 and 14 day exercise reviews (#358) are events too; the exercise text is not sent. Migration 035 (a check constraint only). `docs/changes/403-calendar-exercise-reviews.md`.
- **#365 Reminder email through AWS SES (PR #400).** A job inside the API process at 08:00 Asia/Seoul sends one email on a day with study log reviews or exercise reviews due, none otherwise. One row per day in `reminder_sends` (migration 034) keeps it to one email a day even with a restart or a second container. It is off unless the sender and receiver addresses are set. New package `@aws-sdk/client-sesv2` 3.1176.0 (approved install, package review in the PR), so every checkout needs `npm ci` after this merge. `docs/changes/365-review-reminder-email.md`.
- **#393 Exercise review rebuilt from attempts (PR #402).** Found in the lane 3 backend review. Deleting an attempt left its review due, and an attempt entered with an earlier date was applied as the newest. The review is now rebuilt by replaying the exercise's attempts oldest first, after an add or a delete (user decision A; chapter 7 of `docs/decisions/review-rules.md` says so now). The CI run caught a wrong test of mine, and a self-review round changed the row lock to `FOR NO KEY UPDATE` to avoid a deadlock between two simultaneous attempts. `docs/changes/393-exercise-review-replay.md`.
- **Lane 4 screen fixes, first round:** #394 coding page overflow (PR #397), #395 schedule checkbox touch target (PR #399), #396 readable text sizes in text areas, labels and badges (PR #401).
